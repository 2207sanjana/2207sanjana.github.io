About

I'm an engineer from Hyderabad, India, now based in Kassel, Germany pursuing my Master's in Electrical Communication Engineering. My interests span the full ECE landscape — from writing low-level embedded C to designing digital circuits in Verilog on FPGA hardware.

Before starting my Master's, I spent a year at PeopleTech Group on a General Motors project — developing C and C++ software for automotive display systems, working in a safety-critical environment with tools like Qt Creator and IBM Rhapsody.

I've also participated in two Erasmus+ Youth Exchange programmes across Europe, which sharpened my cross-cultural communication and teamwork skills significantly.

I'm currently open to Werkstudent, internship, or entry-level opportunities in Germany across embedded systems, electronics engineering, telecommunications, IoT, or automotive software.
